## What we learned from this

**Useful dimensions we adopted:**
- Pathway granularity (fresh vs aged, specific application methods)
- The "what happens when you stop" question
- Women's menstrual/hormonal observations
- Lab test sharing with consent tiers
- Follow-up contact option

**Framing differences — where our approach diverges:**
- Their Section 2 is titled "Health Changes & Benefits" — leading language
  that presumes positive outcomes. Our template asks "what did you observe."
- "Would you recommend urine therapy to others?" is prescriptive territory.
  Our archive does not ask this.
- "Most obvious positive changes" — again, leads the answer. We ask for
  changes, non-changes, and surprises equally.
- No concurrent factors section. They never ask what else was happening in
  the contributor's life. Our template makes this mandatory.
- Their data is closed. Ours is open.
