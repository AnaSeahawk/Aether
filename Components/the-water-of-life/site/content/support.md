title: Support the archive
---

The Water of Life is independent, unfunded, and open source. There is no
institution behind it, no grant, no sponsor. It runs on one person's time
and the conviction that this data should exist and be accessible.

Running costs are near zero — the archive is hosted free on GitHub. What
takes time is stewardship: reviewing contributions, transcribing interviews,
maintaining the methodology, and keeping the archive clean and searchable.

If this work has been useful to you, or if you believe the dataset should
exist whether or not you use it yourself, you can contribute to keeping
it alive.

## Where the money goes

- Domain and hosting costs
- Transcription tools for audio interviews
- Stewardship time
- Growing the archive's reach so more people can contribute

This page will be updated as the archive grows and its needs change.
