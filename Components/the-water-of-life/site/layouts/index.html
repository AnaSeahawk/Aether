The Water of Life

An open-source, participatory observational archive collecting first-person accounts of engagement with auto-urine practice.

No claims. No diagnoses. No prescriptions. Just the data.

What this is

This archive holds self-reported experiences from practitioners, in their own words, alongside everything else that was happening in their lives at the time. It does not interpret, diagnose, or evaluate. It collects.

As of August 2026, no controlled clinical trial on auto-urine therapy in humans has been conducted. The institutional dismissal is based on the absence of evidence, not on evidence of absence. The historical record spans five thousand years. The structured dataset of self-reported experiences does not exist. This archive builds it.

Contribute

Your experience matters — not because it proves anything, but because it exists. You can contribute in two ways:

  • Fill out the template — a structured set of questions you can answer in writing
  • Request an interview — a recorded conversation, transcribed and formatted into an archive entry

You choose how you are identified: real name, pseudonym, or fully anonymous.
