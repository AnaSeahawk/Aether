### Fertilizer Essay

The piece has a strong public essay arc, but it needs containment before it belongs in SB:

1. Keep the opening through the phosphorus loop as the core doorway.
2. Move the "fresh or aged" material out of the main essay flow or rewrite it as "variables that change soil use" rather than instructions.
3. Recast the "how it's actually done" section as non-instructional analysis, or remove it from the SB version entirely.
4. Replace direct reader-facing language with archive language. The SB archive records and interprets; it should not tell the reader what to do with their body, garden, or food plants.
5. Tighten evidence claims before review: "safest fertilizer," "healthy body essentially sterile," "400 grams of wheat," pharmaceutical uptake, crop-yield comparisons, and regulatory approval all need specific sources if the piece moves toward public visibility.
6. Keep the disgust/waste-language close. That is the strongest SB bridge: the piece connects directly to the archive's larger argument that naming determines what can be perceived.

Best revised flow:

1. The substance being misnamed.
2. The phosphorus/fertility loop.
3. What urine contains.
4. Why "waste" is a perceptual error.
5. What field research and approved products show.
6. What remains bounded or unresolved: pathogens, pharmaceuticals, storage, crop type, regulation.
7. Closing on fertility as a returned relationship, not a personal instruction.
