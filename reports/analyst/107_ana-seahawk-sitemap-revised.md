## Why a revision

There were two maps, answering different questions:

- **September 26, *Website and Offering Map*** (Drive): how people take part. Its top menu is Home, Explore, Gather, The Living Year, Work with Ana, Site Map.
- **October 14, Report 106**: what is there. It gives the subject tree: Ways of Living, Sovereign Biophysics, The Living Record, The Living Year, private calls, and the separate sites.

They fit together. The September choices become the top menu. The October subject tree fills Explore.

## Decisions taken with Ana

- **Ways of Living** is the section name. "Living Design" stays only as lineage: *Biophilia Living Design* (2016–2021) is named inside the Biophilia and the Vivarium pages.
- **Urbaculture** is the spelling ("urban culture", after Ana's 2017 *#UrbaCulture*). It is the lived mirror of Sovereign Biophysics.
- **Structure Follows the Ground**, the foundation essay, is the page behind the Ways of Living door.
- **Habitat Reading and Habitat Design** are both writing and a service. The writing sits under Ways of Living as short intros that hold the space. The service sits under Work with Ana. Each intro may name the service in one plain line.
- **The Living Year** is one door holding both the writing about time and the ways to take part. Two doors with one name would confuse visitors.
- **The Shivambu Women's Circle** gets its own site. It is held by Ana, Fiona and Megan together, and its own space lets it belong to all three.
- **The ontology is not a door.** It runs underneath, in tags and links.
- **The sitemap becomes a real page** on the website, with each destination clickable.
