## Where the existing art earns its place

- **About and Foundations.** About’s photographs already punctuate shifts in the story, including the house and the road. The linked Foundations essay can carry its own reading rhythm. Prioritize the practical navigation cards before inserting art into either.
- **Place Readings, Urbaculture and A Website of One’s Own.** These are reference pages for three distinct uses of imagery: explanatory sequences, a recognizable destination with richer art inside, and illustrated gestures between people.
- **The Living Year, its Circle pages and Phoenix.** Keep the seasonal marks and the diagrams that explain timing. Where adjacent text describes a diagram already on the page, first try bringing the two together.
- **Writing, Conversations and the homepage.** Preserve the personal photography, the topic-specific illustrations and the approved room marks. The site's warmth already comes through in the mix of a real person, a gathering, and a drawing with a clear purpose.
- **Sovereign Biophysics.** Keep its established entry emblem and the individual art attached to its pieces. This review covered site structure and reading density there. Length alone gives no reason to replace or add essay artwork.

## Scope and order

The review checked the public website checkout at `2eaa49c` and opened its 33 non-redirect HTML routes on the live site at a 390-pixel phone width. All returned HTTP 200. Seven legacy redirect documents, external sister sites, and unpublished materials fall outside that count. The coverage pass inspected page structure, headings, links and visual breaks. The homepage, Writing, Ways of Living and Conversations cards received closer visual inspection. These recommendations describe future previews.

First preview the four Ways of Living cards with existing art. Next compare the matching Shivambu row. A different portrait or a newly drawn gesture can wait until Ana chooses the subject and looks at the brief.
